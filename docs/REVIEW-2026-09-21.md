## Remaining limitations

This review does not turn estimates into official schedules. The application still needs refreshed official inputs when they become available: 2026 postseason first-pitch times and matchups, flexible NFL dates and Westwood One assignments, Stanford/Cal kickoff times, complete Earthquakes fixtures, future MLB/NFL schedules, and postseason/bowl qualification. Basketball and hockey are intentionally outside the three requested sections. These items remain visibly documented in `docs/LIMITATIONS-NEXT.md` and the Needs Review tab.
